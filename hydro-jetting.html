How Jetting Works

How High-Pressure Water Jetting Works

Hydro jetting uses specialized pumps that propel water at up to 4,000 PSI through reinforced flexible hoses tipped with precision 360-degree rotating nozzles. While forward-facing jets puncture stubborn blockages, rearward-facing propulsion jets pull the hose forward through the pipe while scrubbing mineral scale, bio-sludge, and grease off the entire interior circumference.

Why Hydro Jetting Outperforms Traditional Snaking

Standard mechanical drain cables simply poke a narrow hole through soft grease or root tangles. Within weeks or months, grease re-solidifies and tree roots regrow into the existing opening. Hydro jetting completely shears roots flush against the pipe inner walls and washes accumulated sand out to the municipal main or septic inlet.

Ideal Applications in Naples Properties

  • Heavy Grease Blockages: Scours kitchen grease lines in Naples restaurants, condominiums, and rental homes.
  • Fine Sand & Shell Sludge: Flushes out heavy coastal sand that settles in flat-pitch laterals.
  • Tree Root Clearing: Slices ficus and palm root masses flush with pipe walls without damaging structurally intact pipe.
  • Commercial Drain Maintenance: Scheduled preventive scouring for grocery stores, dining establishments, and hotels.
