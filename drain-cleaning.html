Service Details

Targeted Solutions for Household Clogs

Slow or gurgling drains inside your home usually stem from localized debris accumulating inside the fixture P-trap or horizontal branch arm. In bathroom drains, soap scum and hair weave together into dense mats. In kitchen sinks, cooking oils, grease, and food fragments cling to pipe walls, trapping solids until water can no longer pass freely.

Specific Fixtures We Clear

  • Kitchen Sink & Garbage Disposal Lines: Mechanical augering clears solidified cooking grease, starches, and fibrous foods from 1.5” to 2” drain branches.
  • Shower & Bathtub Drains: Motorized cables with specialized bulb heads extract hair masses tangled around mechanical stoppers and P-traps.
  • Bathroom Sinks & Vanities: Disassembly of pop-up drain assemblies and P-traps to remove soap sludge and dropped personal items.
  • Washing Machine Standpipes: Heavy lint accumulations from laundry rinse cycles are retrieved to prevent floor overflows during spin cycles.

Why DIY Chemical Drain Cleaners Cause More Damage

Liquid chemical drain cleaners use sulfuric acid or sodium hydroxide to dissolve clogs. When sitting in standing water, these chemicals produce intense thermal reactions that can warp PVC pipes, dissolve rubber slip-joint washers, and corrode aging cast iron. If the clog is not cleared, you are left with a fixture full of hazardous toxic liquid that creates severe burn hazards for anyone working on the line.
