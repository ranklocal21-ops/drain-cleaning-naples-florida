Main Line Details

Recognizing a Main Sewer Lateral Blockage

Unlike a single blocked sink or shower trap, a main sewer line blockage affects your entire home's drainage infrastructure. All wastewater from toilets, sinks, showers, and laundry appliances converges into one 4-inch or 6-inch pipe running out to the municipal sewer or private septic tank. When this shared pipe is obstructed, wastewater has nowhere to go and rises into the lowest fixtures in the building.

Common Signs of a Mainline Problem

  • Simultaneous Backups: Water rises in showers or floor drains when a nearby toilet is flushed.
  • Gurgling Sounds: Air bubbles force their way through toilet bowls when laundry empties.
  • Sewage at Outdoor Cleanouts: Wastewater pools around exterior white PVC sewer caps.
  • Foul Odors Outdoors: Strong sulfur or sewage smells near driveway edges or yard beds.

Heavy-Duty Cabling & Root Cutting Equipment

Clearing a 4-inch or 6-inch underground lateral requires industrial drum cabling machines with thick 3/4-inch inner-core steel cables. We attach heavy C-cutter and spade blades that spin through dense tree root tangles and compacted toilet paper masses. Once the initial blockage is relieved, we run a video camera inspection to confirm full clearance.
