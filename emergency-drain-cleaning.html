What Constitutes a Real Drain Emergency?

While a slow vanity sink can wait for regular appointment hours, raw wastewater backing up into bathtubs or overflowing toilet bases presents an active biohazard that demands immediate intervention. Contaminated blackwater contains pathogens that saturate flooring, damage drywall, and jeopardize family and customer health.

Immediate Steps While Waiting for Dispatch

  • Cease All Water Usage: Do not run dishwashers, washing machines, or flush any toilets on the property.
  • Turn Off Fixture Isolation Valves: If an individual toilet is overflowing, rotate the oval shutoff valve behind the bowl clockwise until tight.
  • Keep People & Pets Away: Block off affected bathrooms to avoid tracking contaminated water onto carpeted areas.
  • Do NOT Pour Chemical Cleaners: Chemicals cannot clear a submerged mainline and create noxious fumes and skin hazard risks.
