Our Story

Our Approach to Drain & Sewer Cleaning

At Naples Drain Experts, we focus on one essential area of plumbing: resolving drain blockages and sewer flow problems. Plumbing is full of broad generalists, but drain lines and sewer laterals present specialized underground challenges requiring dedicated diagnostic cameras, heavy drum cabling augers, and high-pressure water jetting machines.

We operate on a straightforward philosophy: diagnose the cause first, explain the options clearly, and provide upfront pricing before any authorized work begins. We avoid high-pressure sales tactics or temporary chemical treatments that fail to resolve the underlying restriction.

Demonstrable Service Principles

  • Accurate Assessment: We evaluate the affected fixture, test adjacent lines to verify whether the clog is localized or in a shared lateral, and run video inspection equipment when deeper line faults are suspected.
  • Appropriate Cleaning Methods: We select the tool that matches the blockage—whether that means an auger for localized hair, rotary blades for tree roots, or high-pressure water jetting for heavy sand sludge and commercial grease.
  • Respect for Customer Property: We work cleanly. When entering living areas, we use protective drop cloths, shoe covers, and wipe-down procedures to ensure your home remains tidy.
  • Clear Written Estimates Before Work Starts: You receive an upfront explanation of what needs to be done and the exact price before you authorize our technicians to start.
