Inspection Details

Eliminate Guesswork with Real-Time Video Footage

Before digging up your driveway or authorizing expensive repairs, you need visual certainty. Our waterproof fiber-optic sewer cameras are pushed directly through exterior cleanouts into your main sewer lateral, displaying crystal-clear color video on a portable digital monitor.

What Video Camera Inspections Reveal

  • Tree Root Intrusion: Exact entry points of aggressive Ficus and Royal Palm root systems at unsealed joints.
  • Pipe Bellies & Sags: Sections where pipes dip due to shifting Florida sandy soils, creating standing wastewater pools.
  • Cast Iron Corrosion & Channeling: Heavy scale ridges or missing pipe bottoms in homes built before 1980.
  • Crushed or Separated Lines: Displaced PVC joints caused by heavy vehicles or settling pool patios.

512 Hz Radio Sonde Depth Locating

Our camera heads contain built-in 512 Hz radio transmitters. As the camera travels through the pipe, a technician above ground uses an electromagnetic locator wand to pinpoint the exact physical location and depth down to the inch. If a repair is needed, we excavate only the exact damaged spot rather than digging up an entire lawn.
